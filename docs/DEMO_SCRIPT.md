# Demo Script — Vehicle Selector Pro

Professional voiceover narration for the 2.5-minute product walkthrough.
Record this script over screen captures of the running application.

---

## Recording setup

- **Duration:** 2 minutes 30 seconds
- **Tone:** Confident, clear, professional — like a Shopify Partner demo video
- **Pacing:** Moderate — pause briefly between sections
- **Tools:** OBS Studio (screen record) + any mic, or Loom for quick capture

---

## Scene 1 — Opening (0:00 – 0:10)

**[Screen: Admin dashboard overview]**

> Vehicle Selector Pro is a Shopify application for automotive and specialty-parts merchants. It lets customers find the right parts for their exact vehicle — year, make, model, trim, and engine — and gives merchants a complete fitment management system.

---

## Scene 2 — Admin dashboard (0:10 – 0:25)

**[Screen: Dashboard with catalog stats]**

> The merchant dashboard shows catalog coverage at a glance — how many products are mapped, how many vehicles are in the database, and which fitment rules still need attention.

---

## Scene 3 — Storefront selector (0:25 – 0:50)

**[Screen: Storefront with cascading Year → Make → Model dropdowns]**

> On the storefront, the customer picks their vehicle. Year cascades into Make, then Model, then Trim and Engine. Every selection narrows the results instantly — no page reloads, no waiting.

---

## Scene 4 — Search results (0:50 – 1:05)

**[Screen: Filtered collection page]**

> When they search, the collection filters to compatible parts only. This uses Shopify's native metafield filtering — fast, cacheable, and under fifteen milliseconds per query.

---

## Scene 5 — My Garage (1:05 – 1:20)

**[Screen: My Garage widget with saved vehicles]**

> My Garage lets shoppers save multiple vehicles. A household with a truck and a sedan can switch between them without starting over. The garage persists across pages and sessions.

---

## Scene 6 — Product page: exact fit (1:20 – 1:35)

**[Screen: Product page with green "Guaranteed Exact Fit" badge]**

> On the product page, a fitment badge evaluates compatibility in real time. This intake is a guaranteed exact fit for a 2024 Ford F-150 Lariat — green badge, installation notes, all clear.

---

## Scene 7 — Product page: no fit (1:35 – 1:50)

**[Screen: Product page with red "Does NOT Fit" badge]**

> Incompatible parts fail loudly. This Mustang exhaust does not fit the F-150. The customer sees a clear warning before they add to cart — reducing returns and support tickets.

---

## Scene 8 — Product page: universal (1:50 – 2:00)

**[Screen: Product page with blue "Universal Fit" badge]**

> Universal accessories still sell. LED pods and toolkits show a universal fitment badge — no vehicle required, no confusion.

---

## Scene 9 — Fitment rules (2:00 – 2:12)

**[Screen: Admin fitment matrix / rules table]**

> Merchants manage fitment rules in the admin. Every product-to-vehicle mapping is a row in the matrix. Rules ship live. Incomplete data stays flagged for review.

---

## Scene 10 — Vehicle library (2:12 – 2:22)

**[Screen: Vehicle library / YMMTE explorer]**

> The vehicle library is the normalized cache behind every dropdown. Thirty-three vehicles, thirty-five fitment records — all indexed for fast cascading queries.

---

## Scene 11 — Sync and security (2:22 – 2:30)

**[Screen: Sync monitor or code view]**

> Fitment data syncs to Shopify Product Metafields through GraphQL in batches of twenty-five. Every App Proxy request is verified with HMAC-SHA256. Production ready.
